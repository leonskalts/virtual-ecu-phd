# Algorithmic novelty gate: NO-GO

**NO-GO for a novelty claim.** The implemented reference oracle is exhaustive finite observation equivalence and singleton classification. The measured frontier shows conditional observability, observation cost and unresolved provenance obligations; it does not demonstrate a new computational mechanism, better complexity or practical advantage over established methods. Successful tests do not establish publication novelty.

## What existing methods already cover

| Baseline | Relevant existing capability | Required comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Twin-plant / paired-execution diagnosability | Compare compatible observations from differing fault histories. Polynomial diagnosability tests already avoid constructing a full diagnoser. | Expanded finite product with the same bounded semantics, origin domains and observations. Our two-world search is a baseline application, not itself a contribution. |
| Sensor selection / activation | Choose observations subject to diagnosis requirements and costs. Minimum-cardinality selection for diagnosability is already computationally hard. | Enumerate the same observation policies/contracts; use identical target distinctions and cost definition. |
| Conflict learning | Record causes of failed assignments and avoid repeating related search. | Ordinary nogoods over acquisition/protection choices, including all relevant origin-pair and time variables. |
| SMT/CEGIS | Encode bounded executions/choices, ask for a violating counterexample, refine candidates. | A direct bit-vector/timing encoding with the same admissible domain and UNKNOWN semantics. |
| Automata minimization | Merge states preserving future observable behavior. | Minimize the same deadline-bounded checker; do not compare an optimized proposal to an unnecessarily unminimized implementation. |
| Diagnosis with unreliable sensors | Model observation failures explicitly rather than assuming perfect sensing. | Prior work already supports broad sensor unreliability constraints; origin-dependent evidence failure needs a demonstrated distinction, not a renamed assumption. |

This is a targeted comparison, not an exhaustive systematic novelty review. Statements about our opportunity below are proposals/inferences, not findings of these papers.

## Falsifiable candidate to investigate, not a supported novelty claim

1. **Exact input/output.** Input: a finite timed transition relation, horizon H, origin-dependent writable-domain relation W(o,t), evidence dependencies with acquisition/publication times, a finite menu of implemented acquisition contracts with verified integrity predicates, mandatory origin-pair distinctions and integer cost weights. Output: a minimum-cost feasible contract/checker pair (cost = weighted field acquisitions + reachable minimized checker states), or INFEASIBLE, with a replayable equal-history witness for each unsatisfied distinction. The checker maps prefixes to a specific origin only when all admissible worlds agree; otherwise UNKNOWN.
2. **Method to improve.** Exhaustive contract enumeration, explicit twin-product construction for each contract, followed by output-preserving checker minimization. Also compare against incremental SMT/CEGIS with ordinary learned nogoods. The v7 history trie is only a transparent size proxy, not that strongest baseline.
3. **Tentative distinct mechanism.** Attempt an *origin-pair and deadline indexed antichain of evidence-survival obligations*: a counterexample produces the sets of selected acquisitions and surviving writer domains sufficient to break it at each deadline. Share a time-state product only when its remaining observation suffixes and domain-survival requirements agree for every competing origin, so adding a contract can refine both ambiguity and checker states without rebuilding every product. This is an unimplemented research hypothesis. It may reduce exactly to known conditional hitting sets, belief-state quotienting or conflict learning; no distinction has yet been demonstrated.
4. **Theorem needed.** For the declared finite class, prove the shared quotient preserves the full compatible-origin set for every admissible prefix and contract (including origin-dependent corruption), prove learned obligations are necessary rather than merely sufficient, and prove cost-optimality/completeness. Any claimed asymptotic advantage needs a parameterized separation against the specified baseline; a new data structure alone is insufficient.
5. **Smallest disproof experiment.** Begin with this 134-execution fixture and four policies, then explicitly add a *hypothetical*, separately labeled two-domain write-fault relation and two contract choices. Compare all prefix compatible sets and optimum costs against full enumeration and incremental CEGIS. A single differing compatible set/optimum refutes correctness. Failure to reduce explored product states or total time/memory after overhead on this instance refutes a claimed advantage **on this instance**, not a universal theorem. A two-origin, two-deadline common-cause example is enough to refute an unsound antichain merge.
6. **Missing evidence.** No source-backed arbitrary shared-corruption relation, verified integrity menu, candidate implementation, proof, minimized-checker baseline, scaling study or systematic prior-art separation exists. The current ECU frontier alone cannot establish that the proposed antichain is distinct from standard symbolic product sharing/nogood learning. These are reasons for NO-GO, not license to invent witnesses.

The measurable opportunity is joint observation-contract and checker-size cost under time-varying evidence survival. Current data show reduced observation frequency loses distinctions and that adding timestamps splits constant-value histories. They do not show that any novel optimizer is needed or better. Proceeding would first require a defensible fault-domain/observation contract and stronger baselines, while keeping the current UNKNOWN counterexamples as regressions.
