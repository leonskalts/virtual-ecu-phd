## Separately verified predecessor, not a substitute journal reading

Kallwies, Leucker and Sánchez, ATVA 2022 / [arXiv:2207.05678v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.05678), is an accessible 22-page predecessor with the same title. Its symbolic unfolding accumulates constraints and assumptions; Sections 3–4 treat deductions/pruning, including bounded representations for Boolean and linear-algebra settings. This confirms an earlier collision with generic assumption-aware symbolic monitoring. It does **not** establish the exact added mechanisms or proofs in the 2026 journal article.

## What remains particular to this repository

The source-anchored field/phase audit, honest exclusion of oracle-derived legacy outputs, and experimentally grounded finite ambiguity cases are useful application evidence. They are not an algorithmic separation from the above mechanisms. The v8 reduction proof concerns our specified finite formula, not a claim that all six papers solve precisely the same joint objective.

Direct prior-art reconstruction was sufficient to reject the proposed mechanism's novelty through the first row. Missing Li and journal-SRV full texts are still open obligations; missing access is neither evidence of novelty nor a reason to overstate an equivalence. No author was contacted, no paper was purchased, and no inaccessible full text was guessed from an abstract.
